ℹ️ How This Works
1. How the Data Was Collected
1
Spotify API — Extracted song titles and artist names from hundreds of curated Spotify playlists.
↓
2
Genius Scraping — Automatically scraped full lyrics for each track using the Genius API and HTML scraper.
↓
3
Data Cleaning — Filtered out ~5,000 noisy records: instrumentals, non-English lyrics, and live recordings.
↓
4
GPT Summarization — GPT-5.6 Luna analyzed each song's raw lyrics and generated a 2~3 sentence narrative arc summary + multi-dimensional lyrical theme tags.
↓
5
96-Chip Mapping — Theme tags were mapped into a structured 2-tier ontology: 12 Macro Themes × 8 Micro Emotion Chips.
↓
6
PostgreSQL DB — 23,000+ records loaded into 5 normalized tables on Neon Cloud PostgreSQL with B-Tree indexing for fast retrieval.
2. How the Recommendation Works
1
You select emotion chips — or type a story and let GPT auto-suggest the best chips for your narrative.
↓
2
Stage 1 — Fast SQL Filtering — The selected chip IDs are queried against 23,000+ songs using B-Tree indexed SQL. A Jaccard Purity Score ranks songs where your emotions are the core theme (not just a passing mention). Top 30 candidates retrieved.
↓
3
Stage 2 — LLM Curation — GPT reads the Top 30 candidates' narrative summaries in-context and curates the final Top 3 songs with personalized 1:1 reasons.
↓
4
2nd-Stage Feedback — Not satisfied? Type feedback like "More melancholic" and GPT re-ranks the same 30 candidates with your new direction.
